# Fighter Wu Xia Subclass

## Overview

This mod adds a brand new subclass to the Fighter class in Baldur's Gate 3: **Wu Xia**. Wu Xia combines the essence of Eastern martial arts, featuring unique internal cultivation techniques and external combat skills.

## Class Features

### 2nd Level - Tactical Mind (战术思维)
- **Effect**: When an ability check fails, you can consume a Second Wind use to roll 1d10 and add it to retry
- **Description**: Apply tactical thinking on and off the battlefield, failed attempts don't consume Second Wind

### 3rd Level - Vajra Divine Power (大金刚神力)
- **Effect**: Weapon attacks, unarmed attacks, ranged unarmed attacks, and force damage gain additional damage equal to your Strength modifier
- **Description**: Channel internal energy to enhance attack power, making every strike contain powerful inner force

### 5th Level - Tactical Shift (战术转进)
- **Effect**: Whenever you use a bonus action to regain Second Wind, you can move up to half your speed without provoking opportunity attacks
- **Description**: Perform tactical movement while recovering, demonstrating martial arts agility and lightness skills

### 7th Level - Eagle Eye (鹰眼)
- **Effects**:
  - Gain 60 feet Darkvision
  - Immunity to Blinded condition
  - Increase ranged weapon range by 5 feet
- **Description**: Possess keen vision and perception abilities, able to see clearly in darkness

### 10th Level - Six Meridians Divine Sword (六脉神剑)
- **Effect**: Unlock the legendary Six Meridians Divine Sword skill combination
- **Individual Sword Techniques**:
  - **Shaoshang Sword (少商剑)**: Sharp sword qi, deals 2d20 force damage, **Bonus Action**
  - **Shangyang Sword (商阳剑)**: Precise sword qi, deals 6d6 force damage, **Bonus Action**
  - **Zhongchong Sword (中冲剑)**: Area sword qi, deals 1d20 force damage to enemies within 1.5m radius, **Bonus Action**
  - **Guanchong Sword (关冲剑)**: Powerful sword qi, deals 1d20 force damage, pushes target 3m and may knock prone, **Bonus Action**
  - **Shaochong Sword (少冲剑)**: Swift sword qi, deals 1d20 force damage and grants advantage on next turn's initiative, **Bonus Action**
  - **Shaoze Sword (少泽剑)**: Armor-piercing sword qi, deals 1d20 force damage and inflicts Force Vulnerability, **Bonus Action**
  - **Six Meridians Divine Sword (六脉神剑)**: Ultimate technique, sequentially fires all six sword qi (Shaoze→Shaochong→Zhongchong→Shangyang→Shaoshang→Guanchong), total damage display: 6d20+6d6 force damage, **1 Action, Short Rest recharge**

## Key Features

### ⚡ Enhanced Usability
- **No Usage Limits**: Individual sword techniques have no short rest limitations
- **Bonus Action Economy**: All six individual sword techniques use Bonus Actions
- **Auto-Hit**: All sword qi automatically hit like Magic Missile
- **High Damage**: Significantly enhanced damage values for all techniques

### 🎯 Tactical Flexibility
- **Frequent Usage**: Can use powerful sword qi attacks repeatedly in combat
- **Action Economy**: Bonus Action usage allows for fluid combat flow
- **Combo Potential**: Creates opportunities for consecutive multi-technique usage
- **Authentic Martial Arts Feel**: Matches the "sword qi like rain" descriptions from martial arts novels

## Design Philosophy

- **Martial Arts Style**: Incorporates traditional martial arts novel elements of internal cultivation and sword techniques
- **Balance**: All abilities are carefully designed to maintain game balance
- **Uniqueness**: Compared to Kung Fu Master's Six Meridians Divine Sword, uses fixed damage instead of Ki dice system
- **Progression**: Gradually unlocks more powerful abilities as levels increase
- **High Mobility**: Emphasizes frequent, high-damage output with tactical flexibility

## Installation

1. Copy the entire `FighterWuXia` folder to your Baldur's Gate 3 mods directory
2. Enable the mod in-game
3. Select "Wu Xia" subclass when creating a Fighter character

## Compatibility

- Compatible with the latest version of Baldur's Gate 3
- Can be used alongside other class mods
- Does not affect vanilla game content

## Technical Details

### Damage Summary
- **Shaoshang Sword**: 2d20 force damage (average: 21)
- **Shangyang Sword**: 6d6 force damage (average: 21)
- **Other Four Swords**: 1d20 force damage each (average: 10.5 each)
- **Six Meridians Divine Sword Combo**: 6d20+6d6 total damage (average: 84)

### Special Effects
- **Auto-Hit**: All sword techniques automatically hit targets
- **Force Vulnerability**: Shaoze Sword inflicts Force damage vulnerability
- **Knockback & Prone**: Guanchong Sword pushes and may knock prone
- **Initiative Advantage**: Shaochong Sword grants next turn initiative advantage
- **Area Effect**: Zhongchong Sword affects 1.5m radius
